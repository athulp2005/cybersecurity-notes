# Introduction to Cybersecurity

## Overview

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from cyber threats. As technology becomes a part of everyday life, securing digital information has become essential for individuals, businesses, and governments.

Cybersecurity is not only about preventing attacks but also about detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents.

---

## Learning Objectives

After studying this topic, you should be able to:

- Understand what cybersecurity is.
- Know why cybersecurity is important.
- Identify common cyber threats.
- Understand the relationship between attackers and defenders.
- Recognize how cybersecurity affects daily life.

---

## What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the process of protecting digital systems from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft.

It includes protecting:

- Computers
- Mobile devices
- Networks
- Applications
- Cloud services
- Personal and organizational data

The main goal is to ensure that information remains secure and available to authorized users.

---

## Why is Cybersecurity Important?

Today, almost everything depends on digital technology.

Examples include:

- Online banking
- Social media
- Online shopping
- Healthcare systems
- Educational platforms
- Government services

Without proper cybersecurity, attackers may steal sensitive information, interrupt services, or cause financial and reputational damage.

---

## Real-World Examples

### 📱 Social Media Account

A fake login page tricks a user into entering their username and password, allowing an attacker to take control of the account.

---

### 🏦 Online Banking

A phishing email convinces a user to enter banking credentials on a fake website, leading to financial loss.

---

### 🏥 Healthcare

A ransomware attack encrypts hospital systems, preventing access to patient records until a ransom is paid.

---

## Cybersecurity Goals

A strong cybersecurity strategy aims to:

- Protect sensitive information
- Prevent unauthorized access
- Detect security incidents quickly
- Respond to attacks effectively
- Recover systems after an incident

---

## Attackers vs Defenders

Cybersecurity involves two main groups.

### Attackers

Attackers attempt to exploit vulnerabilities to gain unauthorized access, steal information, or disrupt systems.

### Defenders

Defenders protect systems by monitoring networks, fixing vulnerabilities, and responding to cyber threats.

Cybersecurity is an ongoing effort where defenders continuously improve security while attackers search for new weaknesses.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Cybersecurity protects digital systems and information.
- Modern organizations depend heavily on cybersecurity.
- Cyber threats can affect individuals, businesses, and governments.
- Both prevention and response are important parts of cybersecurity.
- Understanding cybersecurity fundamentals is the first step toward becoming a security professional.

---

## Summary

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital assets from cyber threats. As our dependence on technology continues to grow, cybersecurity plays an essential role in ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information. Learning these fundamentals provides the foundation for understanding more advanced cybersecurity concepts.
