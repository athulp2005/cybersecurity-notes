# Types of Hackers and Terminologies

## 📖 Overview

Hackers are individuals with technical knowledge who interact with computer systems and networks. Their intentions and activities can vary. Some hackers help improve security by identifying vulnerabilities, while others exploit weaknesses for personal gain or malicious purposes.

Understanding the different types of hackers and common cybersecurity terminology is one of the first steps in learning cybersecurity.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this topic, you should be able to:

- Identify the different types of hackers.
- Understand the purpose and motivation of each type.
- Become familiar with common cybersecurity terms.
- Recognize how hacker classifications relate to cybersecurity.

---

## 👨‍💻 Types of Hackers

### 🟢 White Hat Hacker

White Hat Hackers are ethical security professionals who identify vulnerabilities with proper authorization.

**Characteristics**

- Work legally with permission
- Help organizations improve security
- Perform penetration testing
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly

---

### 🔴 Black Hat Hacker

Black Hat Hackers perform unauthorized activities for malicious purposes.

**Characteristics**

- Unauthorized access
- Data theft
- Financial gain
- Malware distribution
- Service disruption

---

### ⚪ Grey Hat Hacker

Grey Hat Hackers operate between White Hat and Black Hat hackers.

They may discover vulnerabilities without permission but generally do not intend to cause harm. They often disclose vulnerabilities after discovering them.

---

### 👶 Script Kiddie

A Script Kiddie has limited technical knowledge and relies on existing tools or scripts created by others.

**Characteristics**

- Beginner-level knowledge
- Uses publicly available tools
- Limited understanding of how attacks work

---

### 🎯 Hacktivist

Hacktivists perform cyber activities to support political, social, or ideological causes.

Their actions may include:

- Website defacement
- Information disclosure
- Service disruption

---

### 🏢 Insider Threat

An Insider Threat refers to an individual within an organization who intentionally or unintentionally compromises security.

Examples include:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Third-party vendors

---

## 📚 Common Cybersecurity Terminology

### Vulnerability

A weakness in a system that can be exploited.

---

### Threat

Anything that has the potential to cause harm to a system or organization.

---

### Risk

The possibility that a threat will successfully exploit a vulnerability.

---

### Exploit

A method or technique used to take advantage of a vulnerability.

---

### Payload

The component of an attack that performs the intended malicious action after successful exploitation.

---

## 🌍 Real-World Example

A company hires an ethical hacker to perform a penetration test.

- The **White Hat Hacker** identifies security weaknesses.
- The organization fixes those weaknesses before a **Black Hat Hacker** can exploit them.

This proactive approach improves the organization's security posture.

---

## 💡 Key Points

- Not all hackers have malicious intentions.
- Ethical hackers help improve cybersecurity.
- Black Hat Hackers perform unauthorized attacks.
- Understanding cybersecurity terminology is essential for learning advanced topics.
- Identifying threats and vulnerabilities helps build stronger defenses.

---

## 📌 Summary

Hackers can be classified based on their intentions, authorization, and objectives. White Hat Hackers work ethically, Black Hat Hackers perform malicious activities, and Grey Hat Hackers operate between these two categories. Understanding common cybersecurity terminology provides the foundation for learning more advanced cybersecurity concepts.
