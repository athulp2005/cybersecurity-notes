# Red Team vs Blue Team

## 📖 Overview

Red Team and Blue Team represent two important roles in cybersecurity. They help organizations evaluate and improve their security posture by simulating attacks and strengthening defenses.

The Red Team thinks like an attacker, while the Blue Team focuses on defending systems against cyber threats.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this topic, you should be able to:

- Understand the purpose of Red Team and Blue Team.
- Identify the responsibilities of each team.
- Compare offensive and defensive security.
- Understand how both teams improve cybersecurity.

---

## 🔴 Red Team

The Red Team is responsible for simulating real-world cyber attacks to identify weaknesses in an organization's security.

### Responsibilities

- Perform penetration testing
- Identify vulnerabilities
- Simulate attacker techniques
- Test security controls
- Evaluate an organization's ability to detect attacks

### Goal

Help organizations discover security weaknesses before real attackers do.

---

## 🔵 Blue Team

The Blue Team is responsible for defending systems against cyber attacks.

### Responsibilities

- Monitor networks and systems
- Detect suspicious activities
- Respond to security incidents
- Apply security patches
- Improve security policies
- Protect organizational assets

### Goal

Prevent, detect, and respond to cyber threats while maintaining system security.

---

## ⚔️ Red Team vs Blue Team

| Red Team | Blue Team |
|----------|-----------|
| Simulates attacks | Defends systems |
| Identifies weaknesses | Fixes weaknesses |
| Performs offensive security | Performs defensive security |
| Tests security controls | Improves security controls |
| Thinks like an attacker | Thinks like a defender |

---

## 🤝 Purple Team

A Purple Team is created when the Red Team and Blue Team collaborate.

Instead of competing, both teams share knowledge to improve an organization's overall security.

---

## 🌍 Real-World Example

A company hires a Red Team to simulate a phishing attack against employees.

The Blue Team monitors email alerts, detects suspicious activity, responds to the attack, and identifies improvements to prevent similar attacks in the future.

---

## 💡 Key Points

- Red Team focuses on offensive security.
- Blue Team focuses on defensive security.
- Both teams have the same objective: improving security.
- Purple Team encourages collaboration between Red and Blue Teams.

---

## 📌 Summary

Red Team and Blue Team are essential parts of modern cybersecurity. The Red Team identifies weaknesses through simulated attacks, while the Blue Team protects systems by monitoring, detecting, and responding to threats. Working together helps organizations build stronger security.
