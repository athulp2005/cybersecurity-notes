# Introduction to Cybersecurity

## What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, and data from cyber threats such as unauthorized access, malware, phishing, and data breaches.

## Objectives of Cybersecurity

- Protect sensitive information
- Prevent unauthorized access
- Ensure business continuity
- Maintain trust and privacy

## The CIA Triad

The three core principles of cybersecurity are:

- **Confidentiality** – Information is accessible only to authorized users.
- **Integrity** – Data remains accurate and is not modified without authorization.
- **Availability** – Systems and data are available when needed.

## Why is Cybersecurity Important?

Cybersecurity helps individuals and organizations protect their digital assets, reduce financial losses, and defend against cyber attacks.

## Example

A phishing email pretending to be from a bank tries to steal a user's username and password. Cybersecurity awareness helps users recognize and avoid such attacks.
